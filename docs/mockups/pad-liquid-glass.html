Segmented, in Apple's Liquid Glass language

Built from Apple's own description of Liquid Glass: the glass bends light (it magnifies what's behind it rather than just frosting it), has specular rims, lifts and lights up from within under your pointer, materializes instead of fading in, keeps every shape concentric, and reserves tint for the one target you're on. Hover slowly; use "replay" to see it open.

1. Regular glass

Six capsule segments sharing one centre. The target lifts outward, lights up where your pointer is, and takes the system-blue tint. replay open

2. Ring + lens

One continuous glass ring; a thicker glass lens rides along it, snaps to the nearest tool and magnifies it (like the iOS 26 tab-bar drag). replay open

3. Clear glass

Apple's "clear" variant: the desktop dims while the pad is open, the segments are nearly invisible glass, and bold white symbols carry it. Most dramatic. replay open

4. Regular, adapting

Same as 1 over a light window: per Apple, the glass and its symbols flip dark for contrast when what's behind them is bright. This is the same pad, shown adapting. replay open
